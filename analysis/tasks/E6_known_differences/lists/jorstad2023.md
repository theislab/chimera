**Data and circularity.** snRNA-seq (10x Cv3, plus SSv4 for apes) of the middle temporal gyrus (MTG) from human, chimpanzee, gorilla, rhesus macaque and marmoset. These donors and this region are not in our cohorts: our DLPFC data are Ma 2022, our M1 data are Bakken 2021. So `circular = no`. **However, MTG from Jorstad is already used elsewhere in the project as a held-out-region profile reference.** Anyone combining this task with that analysis should keep this in mind: the same MTG data feed both.

## Paper's definitions (quoted)

- **Table S7 legend.** "Gene list of differential gene expression comparing each species to the other four species for each consensus type. The gene list was generated by implementing a pseudobulk approach across individuals with DESeq2 using Ward's test [sic, Wald] on downsampled datasets with a maximum 50 nuclei per cluster per individual per species for Cv3 nuclei using one-to-one orthologous genes. Genes with Bonferroni adjusted p-values less than 0.05 were retained."
- **The text calls these hDEGs.** "Next, we identified species-specialized genes by comparing consensus cell type expression for each species to all other primates. Human consensus types had a broad range (fewer than 100 to over 1000) of statistically significant DEGs (Fig. 5C; Table S7) … Excitatory types in deep layers (IT and non-IT) had the most human-specific DEGs (hDEGs)…"
- **Table S3 legend.** "Gene list of pairwise differential expression comparing human, chimpanzee, and gorilla for each subclass … pseudobulk … DESeq2 … Genes with Bonferroni adjusted p-values less than 0.05 were retained."

## Mechanical rules

1. **`human_specific`.** Every row of Table S7 part 1 with `species == "human"` is kept, with no further filter. The table is already cut by the authors at adjusted p < 0.05. Direction comes from the sign of `log2FoldChange`. `effect` is `log2FC=…`.
   - `cell_type` is the consensus type. It is written as the subclass name used in S3 plus `_<index>`: S7's id `L56ITCar31` becomes `L5/6 IT Car3_1`.
   - `note` carries `subclass=`, `padj=` and a flag `fig5C_FC>1.4=yes/no`. Fig. 5C counts hDEGs with FC > 1.4. The flag only marks rows; nothing is filtered on it.
2. **`human_vs_chimp_divergent`.** Every row of Table S3 with `group_1 == "human"` and `group_2 == "chimp"` is kept. `cell_type` is the subclass. Direction comes from the sign of `log2FoldChange`.

**Sign convention.** A positive log2FC means higher in human. Two checks support this:
- The paper's in-situ validated hDEGs are positive in S7: COL11A1 in Chandelier_1 and DACH1 in L5/6 IT Car3_1 and _2. COL11A1 in Chandelier is also positive in S3 human-vs-chimp.
- On the 35,566 (gene, subclass) pairs present in both S7-human and S3 human-vs-chimp, the signs agree in 99.7% of pairs.

## Checks against the paper text

- **Range of hDEGs per type.** The text says "fewer than 100 to over 1000". The table gives a minimum of 25 (OPC_1) and a maximum of 1,834 (L5/6 NP_2).
- **Deep-layer types have the most hDEGs.** The text names "L5/6 NP_2, L6 CT_1, and both subtypes of L5/6 IT Car3". These are the four largest counts in the table: 1834, 1617, 1529 and 1508.
- **Glia have the fewest hDEGs.** This matches "non-neuronal types had the fewest hDEGs".

## Assumptions and oddities

- **Bonferroni or BH is unclear.** The legend says Bonferroni-adjusted p < 0.05. The `padj` column runs up to 0.04999, with a median of 0.009, so it looks like DESeq2's BH `padj`. Either way, the table is used as published, with no re-thresholding.
- **"Human-specific" means human vs the pooled other four primates.** It is not a phylogenetic test of human ≠ chimp = gorilla = macaque = marmoset.
- **Car3 naming differs.** The Car3 subclass is "L5/6 IT Car3" in S3 and in our `cell_type`, but "L6 IT Car3" in S6. Bakken 2021 M1 also uses "L6 IT Car3".
- **Gene symbols are human.** They are one-to-one orthologs, so `symbol_species = human`.
- **Sets not exported.**
  - S7 chimp-, gorilla-, rhesus- and marmoset-specific DEGs.
  - S3 human-vs-gorilla and chimp-vs-gorilla.
  - These sets exist in the downloaded files if a later step needs them.
- **No conserved counterpart.** The paper provides no conserved/shared list, so there is no `conserved_control` set.
