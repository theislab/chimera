## What exists, and what does not

- **No genome-wide table of species-enriched or depleted genes.** The paper has no such table per subtype or subclass. These genes appear only in figures: Fig. 3C, Fig. 5B, fig. S12A and fig. S14I. Places checked:
  - **PMC tables S1–S10.** They are sample sheets, FOXP2 IHC, FOXP2 peaks and GRN, mouse FOXP2 electroporation DE, disease modules, table S9 and a gene glossary.
  - **Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.6824097.** It holds R and Python scripts only.
  - **The Sestan lab Shiny browser.** It is an interactive viewer only, with no download.
- **The supplementary-materials PDF could not be fetched.** It holds the methods with the DE thresholds. The PMC copy is behind a reCAPTCHA, and science.org returned HTTP 403. So the thresholds behind table S9 are not quoted here.
- **The one published per-gene species × subclass table is table S9.** Its title is "Subclass- and species-specific expression of brain disorder risk genes". The text says: "To gain a more detailed view into the cell type-specific expression of the 586 genes with species-divergent expressions, we performed differential expression analysis across subclasses. Of those genes, 157 (27%) exhibited subclass- and species-specific expression patterns (table S9)." Our parse gives 157 unique genes, which matches.

## Mechanical rules

### A. Table S9

Table S9 is a 0/1 matrix with rows (species, Enriched/Depleted, gene) and 29 subclass columns. Each cell equal to 1 becomes one row.
- **Set** is `<species>_specific`. Human rows go to `human_specific`.
- **Contrast** is `<species>_vs_<other three>`.
- **Direction.** Enriched gives `up_in_<species>` and Depleted gives `down_in_<species>`.
- **No effect size.** The table gives none.
- **Restricted gene universe.** This is **not** a genome-wide list: only brain-disorder risk genes were tested. `note` says so on every row.

### B. Main-text statements (`*_text` sets)

Every main-text sentence that names a gene, a cell type and a species-restricted expression difference is included. Each sentence is quoted below; `note` carries a short locator.
- **Excluded: markers of species-specific subtypes.** These describe a subtype, not DE in a shared cell type: FOS, CD83, CCL3, CCL4, CH25H, CD86, BTG2 and CDKN1A for Micro P2RY12 CCL3; GLDN, MYO1E, PADI2 and PPARG for Micro P2RY12 GLDN.
- **Excluded: TH and SST.** The "human-specific switching" is at the protein level. TH RNA is present in human and macaque SST HGF subtypes.
- **Excluded: FOXP2 in L4.** This is IHC only.
- **Excluded: dopamine-pathway genes listed as merely "expressed".** These are GCH1, SLC18A2 and DRD2.

**Quotes (verbatim from PMC9614553 text):**

`human_specific_text` (contrast human_vs_chimpanzee_macaque_marmoset, all up_in_human):

- **NMU and PENK, inhibitory neurons with subclass not stated.** "genes encoding neuropeptides (e.g., NMU and PENK) and carbonic anhydrases overrepresented in human-enriched genes" (fig. S14I)
- **SLC47A1, SST HGF.** "SLC47A1, which encodes a non-canonical dopamine transporter, was uniquely expressed in both TH-expressing inhibitory neuron subtypes in humans"
- **VEGFB and PGF, PC (pericytes).** "VEGFA was sparsely expressed in pericytes of all species while VEGFB and PGF were enriched in human pericytes (Fig. 5F)"
- **DSCAM, Micro.** "DSCAM … was predicted to be positively regulated by FOXP2 in microglia and exhibited human-specific expression in microglia as well" (fig. S22C–D)
- **MERTK, L3-5 IT, and CACNA1D, Micro.** "…the multiple sclerosis risk gene, MERTK, enriched in human L3–5 IT excitatory neurons and the ASD risk gene, CACNA1D, enriched in human microglia." These genes are also in S9.
- **NR4A2, SMC.** "the ASD risk gene, NR4A2 … exhibited human-specific expression in smooth muscle cells". This gene is also in S9.

`hominini_specific_text` (contrast human_chimpanzee_vs_macaque_marmoset; direction up_in_human, meaning up in human and chimpanzee):
- **CYP26B1 (L5-6 NP and L6 CT) and CBLN2 (multiple ExN subclasses).** "CYP26B1, enriched in Hominini L5–6 near-projecting and L6 corticothalamic subclasses, and CBLN2, globally upregulated in multiple Hominini excitatory neuron subclasses."
- **GBA and GOSR2, subset of ExN and InN subclasses.** "two Parkinson's disease risk genes (GBA and GOSR2, module 22) … displayed Hominini-enriched expression in a subset of excitatory neuron and inhibitory neuron subclasses" (fig. S23D)

`macaque_specific_text`:
- **ANXA3, deep-layer IT, up_in_macaque.** The quote is the ANXA1 sentence above.

`primate_vs_mouse_text` (contrast primates_vs_mouse; direction `up_in_primates`). The mouse data are external, but the primate side is Ma 2022 data, so these rows are circular too:
- **FOXP2, L3-5 IT.** "we also observed a previously unknown enrichment of FOXP2 expression in L3–5 intratelencephalic excitatory neurons across primates, that was not detected in mice (Fig. 6A)."
- **PHLDB2, PLCH1 and IL1RAPL2, L3-5 IT.** "Several genes (PHLDB2, PLCH1 and IL1RAPL2) positively regulated by FOXP2 in L3–5 intratelencephalic excitatory neurons also displayed primate-specific expression in these neurons compared to mice" (fig. S22C–D)

## Assumptions and oddities

- **Direction vocabulary was extended.** It now includes `up_in_<species>`/`down_in_<species>` for non-human species and `up_in_primates`, beyond `up_in_human`/`down_in_human`.
- **Text cell-type names are as written.** Some are broader than a subclass: "upper-layer IT subclasses", "multiple excitatory neuron subclasses", "inhibitory neurons (subclass not stated)". Match them to our DLPFC labels by hand later, before seeing model values.
- **Where the `note` locators come from.** For MELTF, SLC47A1, VEGFB/PGF, DSCAM, PHLDB2/PLCH1/IL1RAPL2, GBA/GOSR2 and FOXP2 (L3-5 IT), the locator is the figure call inside the quoted sentence. For ANXA1/ANXA3, PKD2L1, CYP26B1/CBLN2 and MERTK/CACNA1D/NR4A2, it is the nearest figure or table call in the same paragraph.
- **Gene symbols are as published.** They are human or consensus annotation, including lncRNA IDs such as `AC008697.1` and `RP11-…`. `symbol_species = human`.
